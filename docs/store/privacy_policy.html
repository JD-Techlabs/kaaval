Kavaryn: Guardians of Dawn — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Kavaryn: Guardians of Dawn ("the game") handles, and why. The game is published by JD Techlabs ("we"). Contact: jdtechlabs.support@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The game can be played fully offline. We do not ask you to create an account.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device only.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob and sells optional in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or photos. We do not operate our own servers that store information about you.

Advertising and consent

Ads are shown only when you choose to watch one for a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID to show personalised or non-personalised ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where applicable), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

How Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The game receives only a confirmation of what was bought, so it can deliver the items and restore them if you reinstall.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Keeping and deleting data

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game; doing either deletes it. For data held by Google, use the controls in your Google account and device settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google; you can also contact us at the address above and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the effective date above.
